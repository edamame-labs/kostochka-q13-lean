# 2026-09-06 tutorial-site

## Goal
Build an interactive tutorial explaining the context of KKX Question 13 and our
counterexample, for a reader with undergraduate graph theory only (no prior
exposure to equitable colouring, list colouring, or choosability). Deploy it as a
website on GitHub Pages.

## What was done
- `docs/index.html` — single self-contained page, ten sections, no build step and
  no dependencies beyond a Google Fonts stylesheet. Content is derived from
  `notes/COUNTEREXAMPLE.md`, `notes/STRUCTURE.md` (Prop. 4) and `paper/note.tex`;
  no new mathematics.
  1. proper colouring · 2. equitable colouring · 3. α_v and Theorem 27 ·
  4. lists and choosability · 5. equitable / KPW / SE, and the capacity of k−1
  colours · 6. Question 13 · 7. the counterexample · 8. why q = 2k²+1 ·
  9. the general obstruction and open questions · 10. how it was checked.
- Six interactive labs (vanilla JS, inline SVG): colour a tree; a star lab where
  the independence condition decides K_{1,m}; the K_{2,4} list trap; a referee that
  computes q, cap, n mod* k and the k−1 capacity; the counterexample itself for
  k ∈ {2,3,4} with the capacity buckets overflowing by one; the slack table.
- Static figure in §1 showing a forest with four components (5, 3, 2 and 1
  vertices) beside a 4-cycle marked as a non-example — §1 defined "forest" but only
  ever drew a single tree.
- Static figure `F₂ in full` in §7: all 18 vertices and all 18 lists drawn, since
  k = 2 is the only case that fits on a screen.
- Theme toggle (auto/light/dark, persisted in localStorage), scroll-spy nav,
  reading-progress bar, skip link, print styles.
- `.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes `docs/` on push to `main`
  (actions/upload-pages-artifact + deploy-pages). `docs/.nojekyll` also present so
  a branch-based Pages source would work.
- README: `docs/index.html` added to the contents table, plus a Tutorial section
  with the site URL and the local-preview command.

## Results (with exact commands/outputs for computations)
No new computations. Two derivations that were implicit in the notes are spelled
out step by step on the site, and both were checked against the existing numbers:

- Slack: S := (k−1)q − |A|. From n = 2 + |A| + |B| = kq we get |B| = q − 2 + S,
  and the adversary needs |B| ≥ k²(S+1), so
      q ≥ k²(S+1) + 2 − S = S(k²−1) + k² + 2,
  increasing in S. S = 0 gives α_{u₀} = q − 1 < q (fails the independence
  condition — this is exactly what makes Theorem 27 true). S = 1 gives
  q ≥ 2k²+1, n ≥ 2k³+k. S = k−1 gives q = k³−k+3, i.e. the Kaul–Mudrock–Wagstrom
  parameters, reproduced exactly by the same formula.
- Why the SE "at most n mod* k full classes" clause is vacuous for F_k: n = kq so
  cap = q, and k+1 classes of size q would need (k+1)q > n vertices.

Local check of the built page (JS executed, no console errors): doctype html,
7 lab panels, 5 inline SVG figures, theme toggle cycles auto → light → dark and
repaints the body background, k = 3 buckets render 38 seats + 1 spill cell, and
the §7 verdict reads 37 + 2 = 39 > 38.

## Dead ends / what failed and why
- Drawing every vertex of F_k for general k does not work: |A| = 37 already at
  k = 3. Resolved by drawing B in full always (it is exactly a k×k grid of pairs,
  so 2k² dots) and compressing A to 18 dots with a count, plus the separate
  all-vertices figure at k = 2.
- The first pass laid the two stars side by side; edges from w₀ to the lower row
  of (c,d) cells crossed the upper row. Fixed by stacking the two components.
