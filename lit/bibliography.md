# Bibliography (verified against the sources listed)

- H. A. Kierstead, A. V. Kostochka, Z. Xiang, *Results and Problems on Equitable
  Coloring of Graphs*, arXiv:2504.14711; Sum(m)it 280, Bolyai Soc. Math. Studies 32
  (2026). Question 13 is in §5 after Theorem 27. Plain text can be regenerated with `lit/fetch.sh` (not committed).
- H. A. Kierstead, A. V. Kostochka, Z. Xiang, *Equitable list coloring of sparse graphs*,
  arXiv:2411.08372; Discrete Math. 349 (2026) 114981. §1.2 defines full/overfull/SE.
  Plain text via `lit/fetch.sh`.
- H. A. Kierstead, A. V. Kostochka, Z. Xiang, *Equitable list coloring of planar graphs
  with given maximum degree*, J. Graph Theory 108 (2024) 832–838. (SE introduced; not read.)
- A. V. Kostochka, M. J. Pelsmajer, D. B. West, *A list analogue of equitable coloring*,
  J. Graph Theory 44 (2003) 166–177. Defines equitable k-choosability; forests are
  equitably k-choosable for k ≥ 1 + Δ/2 (as quoted in KMW below; not read directly).
- H. Kaul, J. A. Mudrock, T. Wagstrom, *On the equitable choosability of the disjoint
  union of stars*, arXiv:2008.06333 (2020). Proposition 22: K_{1,(k−1)(k³−k+2)} + K_{1,k³}
  is not equitably k-choosable. Intro quotes: K_{1,m} is equitably k-choosable iff
  m ≤ ⌈(m+1)/k⌉(k−1) (attributed to [14] there).
- B.-L. Chen, K.-W. Lih, *Equitable coloring of trees*, JCTB 61 (1994) 83–87.
- D. Miyata, S. Tokunaga, A. Kaneko, *Equitable coloring of trees*, 1994 manuscript.
- G. J. Chang, *A note on equitable colorings of forests*, European J. Combin. 30 (2009)
  809–812. Short proof of Theorem 27 (reproduced in the KKX survey).
- B. Bollobás, R. K. Guy, *Equitable and proportional coloring of trees*, JCTB 34 (1983) 177–186.
